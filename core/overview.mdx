## How Topics Are Organized

The Job Knowledge Base separates content into three layers so you can always find the right level of detail:

- **Core** contains foundational knowledge — the workflows, principles, and practices that apply broadly across roles and departments. Start here when you want to understand how things work and why.
- **Guides** translate that foundation into practical, task-oriented instructions. Each guide focuses on a specific goal you want to accomplish, walking you through it step by step.
- **Reference** holds supporting material — a glossary of terms, frequently asked questions, and a curated list of external resources — that you can consult whenever you need a quick lookup or deeper context.

Within each section, pages are ordered from introductory to advanced. You do not need to read everything in sequence; instead, use the sidebar to navigate directly to the topic that matches your current question or task.

<Tip>
  If you are new to the Job Knowledge Base, start with [Workflows](/core/workflows) to build a mental model of how work moves through your organization, then visit [Common Tasks](/guides/common-tasks) to put that understanding into immediate practice.
</Tip>
