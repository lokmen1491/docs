This FAQ addresses the questions employees ask most often — both about using the Job Knowledge Base itself and about handling common workplace situations. If you're new to the organization or just need a quick answer, start here. Each question below can be expanded to reveal a full answer and, where relevant, links to deeper resources.

<Accordion title="How do I find information in this knowledge base?">
  Start with the sidebar navigation on the left — it's organized by topic area, from core concepts and workflows to guides and reference materials. If you know what you're looking for, use the search bar at the top of the page and enter keywords related to your question. Results are ranked by relevance, and each result shows the page title and a short excerpt so you can quickly identify the right article. For a high-level map of what the KB covers, visit the [Introduction](/introduction) page.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What should I do if I can't find what I'm looking for?">
  First, try rephrasing your search with different keywords — sometimes a synonym or a more specific term yields better results. If your search still comes up empty, check the [Glossary](/reference/glossary) to make sure you're using the correct terminology. If the information genuinely doesn't exist yet, use the article suggestion process (see "How do I suggest a new article?" below) to flag the gap. In the meantime, reach out to your manager or team lead for direct guidance.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How often is the knowledge base updated?">
  The Job Knowledge Base is reviewed and updated on a rolling basis. Core process documentation is audited at the start of each quarter, while reference materials and guides are updated as policies or workflows change. Each article displays a "last updated" date so you can gauge how current the information is. If you notice content that seems outdated, report it using the feedback mechanism described below.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Who should I contact if I spot an error?">
  If you find incorrect, outdated, or misleading information in an article, use the feedback link at the bottom of the relevant page to flag the issue. Include the article title, the specific text in question, and a brief note describing the error. A Knowledge Base administrator will review and correct the content within five business days. For urgent inaccuracies that could cause operational harm, escalate directly to your manager or the KB owner listed on the [Resources](/reference/resources) page.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I suggest a new article?">
  Submit article requests through your organization's internal request form (linked on the [Resources](/reference/resources) page). In your request, include the topic you'd like covered, why it's needed, and who the intended audience is. Suggestions are reviewed monthly by the KB team, and approved articles are typically published within two to four weeks. If your suggestion is time-sensitive, note that in the request and your manager can help expedite the review.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What do I do on my first day?">
  Your first day is primarily about orientation and access. Start by completing any mandatory paperwork or compliance tasks sent to you before your start date. Then connect with your manager for a welcome meeting, where they'll walk you through your role, immediate priorities, and the team's ways of working. Make sure to set up access to core tools (email, project management, communication platforms) using the instructions in the [Getting Set Up guide](/guides/getting-set-up). Review the [Quickstart](/quickstart) page for a prioritized checklist of your first-week essentials.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I request time off?">
  Time off requests are managed through your organization's HR or workforce management system — typically accessible from your employee portal. Submit requests as early as possible, especially for extended leave or during peak periods. Your organization's leave policy (found in the Employee Handbook on the [Resources](/reference/resources) page) outlines approval windows, accrual rules, and any blackout periods. Always confirm approval with your manager before making travel or personal plans tied to the time off.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where can I find my job responsibilities?">
  Your formal job responsibilities are documented in your Role Description, which should have been shared with you during the hiring process. If you can't locate it, ask your manager or HR representative for the most current version. Day-to-day responsibilities may also be outlined in your team's onboarding documentation, your project assignments, and any OKRs or KPIs set at the start of a review cycle. See the [Key Concepts](/key-concepts) page for more on how roles and responsibilities are typically structured.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I escalate an issue?">
  Escalation is appropriate when an issue is outside your authority to resolve, when a deadline is at risk, or when a decision requires visibility from senior leadership. Start by documenting the issue clearly: what the problem is, what you've already tried, and what you need from leadership. Then bring it to your direct manager first — most issues can be resolved at this level. If the issue spans multiple teams or involves a systemic risk, your manager can escalate further. For a full breakdown of escalation pathways, see the [Workflows](/core/workflows) page.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I access key tools and systems?">
  Access to tools and systems is provisioned by your IT team, typically within your first one to two days. You should receive a welcome email with login instructions for your core platforms (email, calendar, project management, and communication tools). If access to a specific tool hasn't been granted, submit a request through your IT helpdesk portal. For a full overview of the tools your team uses and how to get started with each, visit the [Getting Set Up guide](/guides/getting-set-up) and the [Resources](/reference/resources) page.
</Accordion>
