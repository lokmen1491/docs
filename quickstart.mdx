Getting up to speed with the Job Knowledge Base takes just a few focused steps. This Quickstart walks you through the most important starting points so you can move from orientation to confident, independent use as quickly as possible. Whether you've just been given access or you're returning to explore more deeply, follow these steps in order for the clearest path forward.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Orient Yourself with the Introduction">
    Start by reading the [Introduction](/introduction) to understand the full scope of what this knowledge base offers and how it's organized. The Introduction explains who this resource is built for, breaks down each major section, and sets the context you need to navigate everything else with confidence.

    Pay particular attention to the **What's Inside** section, which maps out the four main areas: Getting Started, Core Topics, Guides, and Reference. Knowing where each type of content lives saves you time every time you return.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Learn the Key Concepts">
    Before diving into workflows or guides, visit the [Key Concepts](/key-concepts) page to build your foundational vocabulary. This page defines the core terms and mental models used throughout the knowledge base — including what distinguishes a *Guide* from a *Workflow*, and what a *Best Practice* means in this context.

    Understanding these concepts upfront means you won't have to pause mid-task to look up terminology. It also makes the Core Topics section significantly easier to absorb.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow Your First Guide">
    Head to [Getting Set Up](/guides/getting-set-up) and work through it step by step. This guide is designed as your first practical exercise — it walks you through configuring your tools, preferences, and workspace so you're ready to use the knowledge base and related systems effectively.

    Once you've completed that guide, browse [Common Tasks](/guides/common-tasks) to see walkthroughs for the most frequent day-to-day scenarios. Each guide is self-contained, so you can jump to whichever task is most relevant to you right now.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bookmark the Reference Section">
    Add the [Glossary](/reference/glossary), [FAQ](/reference/faq), and [Resources](/reference/resources) pages to your browser bookmarks or favorites. These pages are built for repeated use — the Glossary defines every key term, the FAQ answers the most common questions, and Resources links you to supplementary tools and documentation.

    Having these bookmarked means you'll always have a fast fallback when you encounter an unfamiliar term or need a quick answer without reading through a full guide.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  The **search bar** at the top of the page is your fastest path to any answer. Instead of browsing section by section, type a keyword or question directly into search — it scans every page in the knowledge base and surfaces the most relevant results instantly. Use it anytime you know what you're looking for but aren't sure where it lives.
</Tip>
