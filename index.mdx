## How to Get the Most Out of This Knowledge Base

Follow these steps to orient yourself and start finding value immediately.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start with the Introduction">
    Read the [Introduction](/introduction) to understand what this knowledge base covers, how it's organized, and which sections are most relevant to your role. This gives you the lay of the land before diving into specific topics.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Learn the Key Concepts">
    Visit [Key Concepts](/key-concepts) to familiarize yourself with the core terminology and mental models used throughout the knowledge base. Understanding these concepts makes every other section easier to navigate and apply.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow a Guide">
    Head to the [Guides](/guides/getting-set-up) section to work through step-by-step instructions for real tasks. Start with *Getting Set Up* if you're new, or jump to [Common Tasks](/guides/common-tasks) for everyday workflows.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bookmark Your Reference Pages">
    Save the [Glossary](/reference/glossary), [FAQ](/reference/faq), and [Resources](/reference/resources) pages for quick lookups. These pages are designed for repeated use whenever you need a fast answer or a definition on the fly.
  </Step>
</Steps>
